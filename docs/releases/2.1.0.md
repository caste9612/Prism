Prism 2.1.0 tells what each disk is (SSD or hard disk) and, with the login of a ZimaOS NAS, shows its RAID and the health of its disks; network folders can now be added by their path.

## What's new

- **Kind of disk** — every physical disk says whether it is an SSD or a hard disk, with the
  rotation speed when the disk reports it, in the heading of its partitions, on its screen and
  in the *Health* tab; also while the disk is in a drawer.
- **RAID of a ZimaOS NAS** — in *Settings › NAS*, connect a NAS of your catalog with the login
  of ZimaOS. Every shared folder then shows the RAID it is on ("RAID 1 · OK"), the heading of
  the NAS its state, and the *Health* tab of the folder the disks of the RAID: type, size,
  temperature, hours and state ("SMART not available" when the NAS cannot read a disk). The NAS
  is read every hour, and a notification tells you when a RAID or one of its disks gets worse.
  The login is checked by reading the NAS once, then kept encrypted in the Windows Credential
  Manager (*Windows credentials › Prism/ZimaOS/…*); Prism only reads from the NAS and never
  changes anything there.
- **Add a network folder by its path** — *Network folder…* in the catalog bar adds a shared
  folder Prism does not find by itself: one mapped only for the current session, or a folder of
  another PC or NAS (`\\server\folder`). Prism checks that it answers, then proposes to index it.

## Notes

- RAID and NAS disks are read only from ZimaOS; other NAS systems keep working as network
  folders, without their RAID.
- The catalog moves to schema 11 the first time 2.1.0 starts (a new table for the kind of each
  disk); nothing has to be indexed again.

## Install

Prism 2.0.0 updates itself: *Settings › Updates › Install and restart*. Otherwise download
`Prism_2.1.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it (Windows 11, 64-bit; Windows asks for
administrator permission). The installer is not signed yet: if SmartScreen says "Windows
protected your PC", choose *More info › Run anyway*.

## In italiano

Prism 2.1.0 dice che cosa è ogni disco (SSD o disco rigido, con i giri quando il disco li
dichiara) e, con il login di un NAS ZimaOS dato in *Impostazioni › NAS*, mostra il RAID di ogni
cartella condivisa e la salute dei suoi dischi, con una notifica quando un RAID o un disco
peggiora; il login resta cifrato nel Gestore credenziali di Windows e Prism sul NAS legge
soltanto. Con *Cartella di rete…* nella barra del catalogo si aggiunge una cartella di rete con
il suo percorso. Prism 2.0.0 si aggiorna da solo: *Impostazioni › Aggiornamenti › Installa e
riavvia*.
